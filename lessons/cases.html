Interview cases

How to practise

Ten cases like the ones in FDE interviews. Each is a vague request from a client.

For each one: take 5 minutes, then say your answer out loud. Then open the cards and compare. There's no single right answer: interviewers grade how you get there.

Better still, practise with a partner, or with an AI chatbot playing the client.

A prompt for any AI chatbot
You are interviewing me for a forward deployed engineer role. Give me this case: [paste the case]. Play the client's head of operations. Only share facts and numbers when I ask for them, and make them realistic. Push back once on my plan. When I say "done", grade me on: clarifying questions, a clear problem and metric, a small first version, risks and guardrails, and how clearly I explained it. Then give me one thing to improve.
