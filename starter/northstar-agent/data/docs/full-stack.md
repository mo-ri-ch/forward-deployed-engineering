Full Stack Web Development, also called the MERN course, runs for 9 months. You build complete websites with MongoDB, Express, React and Node.

Classes run on campus or hybrid. Mentors answer doubts every evening, and working engineers review your code every week.

The last 2 months are an internship with one of our hiring partners. Some internships are paid and some are not; it depends on the partner.

The fee is ₹1,35,000. You can pay in EMI from ₹11,300 a month.
