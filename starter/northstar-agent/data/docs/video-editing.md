Video Editing & Production runs for 7 months. You learn shooting, editing, colour and sound, and make short films and ads.

Classes run on campus only, because you use the studio.

You need your own laptop for editing at home. Ask the IT team which laptops work well.

The fee is ₹1,25,000. You can pay in EMI from ₹10,500 a month.
