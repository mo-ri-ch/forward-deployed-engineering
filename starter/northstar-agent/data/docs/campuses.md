Northstar has five campuses: Tech Park, City Centre, Riverside, North Hub and Lakeside.

Campuses are open Monday to Saturday, 9:30 am to 6:30 pm. Hybrid students come to campus two days a week.

Counselling calls can be booked with any counsellor, at any campus, by phone or video.
