# Portfolio templates

Turn your Forward Deployed Engineering work into things you can apply with. Copy each template into your own
GitHub repo, replace everything in `<angle brackets>`, and delete the example when you're done.

| Template | What it's for | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Project README](project-readme.md) | The front page of your project on GitHub. Most people only read this. | 1 hour |
| [Demo video script](demo-script.md) | A 3-minute video that shows it working. | 1 hour, plus recording |
| [One-page write-up](write-up.md) | The story of the engagement, for interviewers who want more. | 1 hour |
| [Resume lines and LinkedIn post](resume-and-linkedin.md) | Two or three resume lines, a post, and your two-minute interview story. | 30 minutes |

## One rule: say what's real

If your project used a made-up client or made-up data, like Northstar in this course, say so everywhere:
"on a simulated client with synthetic data". Interviewers respect it, and they will ask. Claiming a real
client you didn't have can cost you the offer. If you ran your capstone with a real organisation, name it only
with their permission.
